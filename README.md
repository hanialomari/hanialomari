## Hi, I'm Hani Alomari 👋

I'm a **Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Virginia Tech**, advised by [Dr. Chris Thomas](https://people.cs.vt.edu/chris/), and a recipient of the **Amazon Fellowship**. I work at the intersection of **computer vision** and **NLP**, studying how vision-language models understand, evaluate, and retrieve images that have more than one valid interpretation.

In Summer 2026, I was a **Machine Learning Engineer Intern at Adobe**, working on image-quality evaluation.

### What I work on
- **Cross-modal retrieval**: matching images and text when meaning goes beyond the literal (metaphor, emotion, context).
- **Visual reasoning & grounding** in vision-language models (VLMs).
- **Image quality & aesthetic assessment**: judging visual content from diverse human perspectives, with interpretable, actionable feedback.
- **Robust retrieval** under noisy and corrupted queries.
- **Acoustic scene mapping**: agents that map rooms from echoes, without cameras or lidar.

### Why it matters
Real-world images are polysemous. Metaphor, emotion, culture, and context often matter more than surface similarity. I build models that capture *all* the valid readings of an image, not just the nearest literal match.

## Selected work
- **[Lenses](https://hanialomari.github.io/lenses/)** (CVPR 2026)
  - A polysemous image-text benchmark (105K images, 732K captions across five interpretive lenses: literal, figurative, emotional, abstract, background) and a lens-token multi-embedding model that roughly doubles figurative image-to-text R@1 over the best zero-shot baseline.
- **[MaxMatch](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1533.pdf)** (ACL 2025)
  - One-to-one set matching (Hungarian assignment) with diversity losses that prevent multi-embedding collapse; state of the art on MS-COCO and Flickr30k without external data.
- **Image-quality evaluation** (Adobe, 2026)
  - A multi-persona annotation pipeline and a DINOv3 + Transformer quantile-regression model that learns quality distributions of visual attributes.
- **Ongoing**
  - Robust retrieval for corrupted text queries, camera-free acoustic room mapping, and latent world models for multi-step composed image retrieval.
